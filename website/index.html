Why We Built Chronon

The Pain of University Timetables

College schedules were an absolute eyesore. One was made in MS Paint, another in Excel with vertical columns so cramped that students literally used a physical ruler on their monitors just to find out what room they were supposed to be in.

The Evil Spreadsheets

The nightmare every university student has suffered through:

  • ✕ Ruler Required: Tiny, unaligned rows that force you to drag your finger or a ruler across the glass.
  • ✕ Zero Real-time Updates: If a lecturer changes rooms, you find out only after knocking on an empty lecture hall.
  • ✕ Horrible on Mobile: Pinching and zooming on a blurry JPEG screenshot downloaded from Facebook groups.
  • ✕ No Conflict Warning: Accidental overlaps between seminar cohorts left undetected until week 3.

The Chronon Solution

Designed for human eyes and high-speed campus routines:

  • ✓ Glanceable Cards: Time, classroom, teacher, and subject in bold, high-contrast, beautiful typography.
  • ✓ Instant Cloud Sync: Instant schedule mutations broadcasted via FastAPI and Supabase backend.
  • ✓ Offline Architecture: Intelligent local caching ensures your timetable is visible even in subterranean basements.
  • ✓ Role-Based Access: Admin controls for schedule directors, clean read views for students, and group filters.
Key Capabilities

Engineered for Academic Precision

Everything you need to master your academic calendar with zero cognitive friction.

Dynamic Visual Timeline

Chronological breakdown of your day with remaining time indicators, current class highlights, and room numbers prioritized for rapid navigation.

RBAC Security & Privacy

Role-Based Access Control protecting personal student information and allowing only authorized academic staff to publish modifications.

FastAPI & Supabase Backbone

Blazing asynchronous Python 3.12 endpoints combined with Supabase PostgreSQL and SQLAlchemy ORM for lightning-quick query responses.

Local + Cloud Double Cache

Minimizes server load while slashing user wait times. If you lose connection in an auditorium, your full weekly schedule remains instant and responsive.

Conflict & Overlap Detection

Automated schedule validation flags collision between concurrent lectures, double-booked professors, or overlapping exam dates.

Cross-Platform Flutter Client

Native-feel experience across Mobile (Android & iOS) and Progressive Web Apps. One responsive codebase with smooth 60fps interaction.
